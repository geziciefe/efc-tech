## Supplied assets inspected

All 39 attachments across the three supplied batches were inspected. The first batch contains twelve 1920×1080 Amron screenshots and the supplied EFC logo. The v1.0.1 batch contains fourteen 1920×1080 comparison screenshots of Amron and the earlier EFC draft. Screenshots are reference-only and have not been copied into the website or archive. No standalone EFC product or operation photograph was present.

The 1254×1254 JPEG `WhatsApp Image 2026-09-09 at 17.41.12 (2).jpeg` is the supplied EFC logo. It is preserved as `public/assets/efc-logo-original.jpg`. The legacy proportional wordmark crop and original favicon are preserved. For v1.1, the original JPEG crop at (55, 215)–(1230, 755) was used to remove its white matte and derive three lossless 1175×540 WebP states. The light and active treatments recolor only the existing wordmark/subtitle, preserving the lens detail. The light, blue/light and original-color states have identical alpha masks (SHA-256 prefix `a0122aeab007ee29`), so the shape and dimensions cannot shift during the crossfade. No logo shape was redrawn. No new imagery was generated. No stock photography or Amron photographic assets are used.

## Typography

Amron's live homepage stylesheet, inspected on 9 September 2026, specifies Montserrat for its body and primary headings, with weights 400 and 600. EFC uses the same font family, obtained separately from Google Fonts, not extracted from Amron. Local font files include Latin and Latin Extended for Turkish. The SIL Open Font License is included alongside the fonts. v1.1 uses larger, more relaxed 600-weight navigation and headings to approach the supplied Amron proportions. The supplied WOFF2 files were confirmed as variable fonts; two range declarations now cover the existing 400–800 weights, without downloading replacement fonts.
